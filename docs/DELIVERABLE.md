## 2. Conversion concept

Primary audience: buyers who want verified Windrose pricing, floor plans and launch information rather than third-party rumours. Offer: email updates when official documents exist. CTA: **Get Project Updates**. Documents are not delivered instantly.

## 3. Landing-page copy

Implemented in the live page components. Primary CTA, form microcopy, consent text, success state, disclaimer and the 10 FAQs are in `lib/site.ts` and `components/`.

## 4. Responsive layout

Desktop: sticky header, hero with form in the right column, 72rem content width, snapshot as a two-column table. Mobile: form under the hero, stacked snapshot rows, sticky bottom CTA above the safe area, 44px-class touch targets, reduced-motion support.

Colours: cream `#F4EFE6`, forest `#2C4A3E`, ink `#1C1A16`, gold `#9D7D45`. Type: Fraunces + Source Sans 3.

Imagery is conceptual Caledon/Ontario photography, captioned as not official renderings.

## 5. Lead form and routing

Visible: first name, last name, email, optional phone, interested-in, optional timing, unchecked consent.

Hidden: page URL/version, referrer, UTMs, gclid/gbraid/wbraid/msclkid/fbclid, form version, consent text/version, timezone.

Validation is inline and server-side. Honeypot submissions return a fake success. Rate limit: 5 posts / 15 minutes / IP. Origin is checked. Failures log to `data/lead-failures.jsonl` without telling the visitor that documents were sent.

CRM mapping: JSON body matches `StoredLead` in `lib/leads.ts`. Retry: webhook is attempted once; failed routing still keeps the captured file.

## 6. Follow-up messages

**On-page success:** “Thank you—your registration has been received. We’ll send verified Windrose at Caledon Trails updates as project information becomes available.”

**Acknowledgement email:** see `acknowledgementCopy()` in `lib/leads.ts`.

**Internal notification:** see `internalCopy()`.

**Optional nurture (only after consent):**

1. Day 0: acknowledgement.
2. Day 7: short note that pricing and floor plans remain TBA, with a link back to the snapshot.
3. On verified document release: one email stating what is now available and that terms can change.
4. Every message includes unsubscribe. Do not pitch mortgages, assignments or unrelated listings.

## 8. AEO / GEO package

Direct-answer block `#direct-answer` (50–80 words). Dated snapshot table. Sources beside consequential facts. Exactly 10 visible FAQs. `public/llms.txt` is provided without any claim that it guarantees citations. Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot and GPTBot are allowed; the owner can disallow GPTBot later without affecting search bots.

## 9. JSON-LD

`WebSite`, `WebPage`, publisher `Organization`, `FAQPage`, `Place` for the project, `ImageObject` for conceptual media. No `Offer`, reviews, ratings, geo-coordinates or inventory.
